# Ledger Market Scan

**Are we onto something?** Competitor and market scan, 2 October 2026.

Four research passes covered Nigeria and Africa, global AI money assistants, ways to get data without statement uploads, and demand signals. Every claim links to its source. Items marked *(unverified)* could not be confirmed directly.

## Verdict: real pain, a real window, and payment still unproven

| Signal | Score |
|---|---|
| Pain is real and measured | **4/5** |
| Feasibility of automatic data | **3/5** |
| Evidence people will pay | **2/5** |
| Before the big AI assistants reach Nigeria | **2 to 4 years** |

The problem is real. Most young Nigerians use several money apps, few track their spending, and three in four say they want one view of everything. The global giants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Meta) just launched money features, but only in the US and only through Plaid, which does not cover Nigerian banks. So there is a window.

**The surprise:** two small Nigerian apps, **Auritrack** and **Budge AI**, are already chasing the same idea. Ledger is ahead on accuracy (reconciliation, no double counting, knowing what payments were for) and on tax. It is not ahead on getting data in automatically. That is the gap to close.

## What the data says about demand

### For it

- **75.2%** of 1,100+ young Nigerians want one dashboard for all their money. Only **5.2%** use a budgeting app, and **36.9%** track nothing. ([Column survey, Jul 2025](https://technext24.com/2025/07/06/23-young-nigerians-save-fintechs/))
- **325.6M** active accounts against **67.8M** BVNs, an upper bound of about 4.8 accounts per person. ([NIBSS, 2025](https://guardian.ng/business-services/bvn-enrolments-hit-67-8m-325m-bank-accounts-active/))
- OPay is on **69%** of smartphones and the top bank app on 16%, so people mix wallets and banks. ([KPMG and Orange, Sep 2026](https://nairametrics.com/2026/09/28/opay-dominates-nigerian-smartphones-as-fintech-apps-outpace-banks/))
- Over **70%** of earners support family. ([PiggyVest, Nov 2024](https://nairametrics.com/2024/11/01/over-70-of-nigerian-income-earners-pay-black-tax-monthly-or-occasionally-report/)) Only **6%** feel financially secure. ([PiggyVest 2025 report](https://blog.piggyvest.com/save/announcement/piggyvest-savings-report-2025/))
- **88%** of Nigerian adults have used an AI chatbot, against a 62% global average. ([Google and Ipsos, Aug 2026](https://www.talktalknigeria.com/home/2026/8/15/88-of-nigerian-adults-now-use-ai-the-highest-rate-on-earth-and-nobody-waited-for-permission))
- OpenAI says **200M+** people ask ChatGPT money questions every month. ([TechCrunch, May 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/15/openai-launches-chatgpt-for-personal-finance-will-let-you-connect-bank-accounts/))

### Against it

- Money apps lose people fast: about **4%** of finance app users remain at day 30, and manual entry is the main reason they leave. ([RevenueCat 2025](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025/))
- Mint (25M users) shut down in 2024 because a free money tracker did not pay. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/select/mint-budgeting-app-is-going-away-here-are-some-alternatives/))
- Okra, Nigeria's open banking pioneer, closed in May 2025. ([Nairametrics](https://nairametrics.com/2025/07/11/inside-the-fall-of-okra-how-africas-most-promising-open-banking-startup-shut-down-at-its-peak/))
- Trust is low: over ₦320B has been lost to digital fraud since 2023, and loan apps that abused people's data made users wary. ([Daily Trust](https://dailytrust.com/nigeria-losing-billions-to-digital-fraud/))
- 43% of Nigerians save nothing, so the paying segment is far narrower than "all smartphone users". ([PiggyVest 2024](https://nairametrics.com/2024/11/01/over-70-of-nigerian-income-earners-pay-black-tax-monthly-or-occasionally-report/))
- No Nigerian data exists on paying for money-management apps.

### The giants moving in, all in 2026

- **ChatGPT Finances** (May 2026): connects US bank accounts through Plaid, read-only, with a tax link via Intuit planned. OpenAI bought Roi and Hiro first. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/15/openai-launches-chatgpt-for-personal-finance-will-let-you-connect-bank-accounts/))
- **Perplexity** (Apr), **Gemini via Google Wallet** (Sep), **Meta Muse** (Sep): all US-only, all through Plaid. ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/), [Plaid blog](https://plaid.com/blog/))
- **Claude Money** was spotted in testing in Sep 2026, not announced. ([gHacks](https://www.ghacks.net/)) *(unverified)*
- **Banks:** Revolut AIR (UK, can act on your account), Bank of America's Erica (50M users), and Nubank's assistant handling 55% of support chats.

**What this means:** in the US, chatting with your bank data is now a free feature of a $20 AI subscription, so a thin "chat with your statements" app can't win there. Every one of these launches depends on Plaid, and Plaid does not cover Nigerian banks, OPay, Moniepoint or Kuda. Ledger's lasting asset is the **local data and tax layer**, which could later plug into those same assistants, as Copilot already does.

## Gaps Ledger can own

1. **One correct total across banks, wallets and currencies.** Auritrack and Budge combine accounts but show no evidence of reconciliation or de-duplication. Ledger already pairs transfers across currencies and checks every running balance.
2. **Knowing what payments were for.** Family support, rent and DD, tithe and informal loans have no structure in any product. Ledger learns them from memos, names, answers and the user's own notes.
3. **Tax prepared from your real transactions.** The Nigeria Tax Act took effect on 1 Jan 2026. Residents are taxed on **worldwide income**, and everyone with income must file by **31 March**; late filing costs ₦100,000, then ₦50,000 a month. Today only calculators exist. ([TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2025/09/29/remote-workers-nigeria-income-tax-2026/), [BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/news/article/personal-income-tax-march-31-deadline-puts-nigerias-new-tax-system-to-test/))
4. **A financial profile you own and share.** A proof-of-income pack for visas, rent and loans. Lenders pull this data today; consumers can't easily push it.
5. **The diaspora corridor.** Naira plus GBP, USD or CAD, remittances and dual tax. Unserved below Kubera's $249 wealth tier. 70% of senders support more than one person. ([Zepz, Aug 2026](https://americanbazaaronline.com/2026/08/20/cross-border-commitments-486715/))

## How to get data automatically

No single route covers Nigeria. CBN open banking is **still not live**: CBN said in Oct 2025 it "hasn't gone live", and a Jul 2026 legal review says it is "not yet fully operational". ([Technext](https://technext24.com/2025/10/20/cbns-open-banking-system-hasnt-launched/), [Legal500](https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/nigeria/finance-and-banking/open-banking-and-digital-lending-in-nigeria-opportunities-risks-and-regulatory-readiness))

| Route | How it works | Cost and rules | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email forwarding | Each user gets an address like alerts+id@ledger. A Gmail or Outlook filter forwards bank alerts automatically | No Google review needed. Works on iPhone and in every country | Build first |
| Gmail connect (built) | One tap, and it can also bring in past emails | Over 100 users needs Google's CASA security audit: about $540 to $1,800 a year, about 2 months ([DeepStrike](https://deepstrike.io/blog/google-casa-security-assessment-2025)) | Start audit now |
| Outlook connect | Microsoft Graph Mail.Read | Users can consent themselves; no audit | Cheap add |
| Android SMS | Reads bank SMS on the phone | Allowed under Google Play's money-management exception. Review is strict, so parse on the device ([Play policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10208820)) | Month 2 to 3 |
| iPhone SMS | Not possible | Apple blocks reading messages | Won't work |
| Mono Connect | Link a bank. Mono has 8M links (about 12% of accounts) and was bought by Flutterwave in Jan 2026 | ₦50k a month for 100 accounts, plus a fee per refresh. Users type their bank login into Mono's own screen | Premium tier |
| WhatsApp bot | Forward alerts or screenshots | Meta bans general chatbots from Jan 2026; a task-specific logging bot is allowed | Month 3 |
| Abroad | Plaid (US), TrueLayer or Salt Edge (UK/EU), Flinks (Canada), Wise tokens, read-only crypto keys | Per-country contracts | By corridor, later |

**Context for Ledger:** banks are pushing customers to **free email alerts** since SMS alerts rose to ₦6 each in May 2025 ([Premium Times](https://www.premiumtimesng.com/business/business-news/791539-gtb-to-begin-charging-n6-per-sms-alert-up-from-n4.html)). Ledger already parses GTBank alerts, so email forwarding is the fastest step away from uploads.

## Who might pay, and how

- **Price anchors in Nigeria:** Spotify ₦1,600, Netflix ₦2,500 to ₦8,500 and ChatGPT Go about ₦7,000 a month. A realistic consumer price is ₦1,500 to ₦3,000 a month (our estimate).
- **Diaspora tier:** $5 to $10 a month is plausible for UK, US and Canada-based Nigerians.
- **Tax season:** a one-off "prepare my return" fee before 31 March, which carries a ₦100k penalty if missed.
- **Profile sharing (B2B):** lenders, landlords and visa agents pay for a verified income pack the user chooses to share. 505 approved loan apps need underwriting data. ([PM News](https://pmnewsnigeria.com/2026/06/27/fccpc-expands-approved-loan-app-list-raises-total-to-505/))
- **The global lesson:** the biggest winners make money from credit or fees, not insight. Cleo earns mostly from advances; Rocket Money from bill negotiation. Insight alone tops out at about 1M payers.

## What could crush this

- **Auritrack and Budge** are already shipping, and Budge's SMS approach moves fast on Android.
- **Flutterwave plus Mono** owns the consent layer and 8M links; a consumer dashboard would be cheap for them.
- **OPay, PalmPay and Moniepoint** own distribution. If one adds "link your other banks", a standalone app struggles.
- **Accuracy is life or death.** One test found ChatGPT got a 200-row statement total wrong in 3 of 5 runs. A wrong tax number is worse than none. Ledger's rule stays: the code computes, the AI explains.
- **Platform risk:** Google can tighten Gmail or SMS rules, and the tax authorities may offer free pre-filled returns.
- **"Global" is the weakest claim.** Lead with Nigeria plus the diaspora corridor, not every bank in every country.

## Next 30 days: how to get a real signal

1. **Recruit 20 to 30 testers.** Each should have 2+ accounts, foreign-currency income or regular family transfers, and track in Notes or Excel or not at all. Find them through Grey, Raenest and Cleva communities, Upwork and Fiverr Nigeria groups, Tech Twitter, and diaspora groups in the UK and Canada.
2. **Ship email forwarding and start the Gmail CASA audit** so testers don't have to keep uploading.
3. **Lead with a tax preview:** "Here's how your 2026 tax looks from your real income." Nobody else offers this, and it has a deadline.
4. **Measure one number:** the share of testers who add a second month or keep alerts flowing for 4 weeks. Above 40% is a strong signal. Also ask "Would you pay ₦2,000 a month?" and offer a pre-order to test it.
5. **Watch Auritrack and Budge.** Sign up for both and note where Ledger is more accurate.
